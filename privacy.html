Privacy Policy

CGMSIM One · Effective date: 23 November 2026

In short: CGMSIM One does not collect, store on any server, transmit, or share any personal data. The app works entirely offline on your device. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Who we are

CGMSIM One is developed by Lorenzo Sandini, Lappeenranta, Finland (“we”, “the developer”). You can reach us at YOUR-EMAIL@example.com.

Data we collect

None. The app makes no network connections. It does not include analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or any other third-party SDKs, and it does not use device identifiers to track you.

Data stored on your device

Settings, simulated patients and simulation results that you create are stored only on your device, inside the app’s private storage. The developer cannot access this data. It is removed when you delete the app. If you have enabled iCloud or device backups, this local data may be included in those backups, which are handled by Apple under its own privacy policy.

Simulated data only

CGMSIM One is an educational simulator. All glucose values and patient scenarios in it are simulated. Please do not enter real patients’ identifying or health information into the app.

Apple diagnostics

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device’s settings, Apple may provide us with anonymised crash reports and usage statistics. This data is collected by Apple, not by the app, and is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy. You can turn this off at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements.

Contacting us

If you email us, we will receive your email address and the content of your message. We use this only to reply to you, do not share it with anyone, and delete it when it is no longer needed. Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) you have the right to access, correct or delete this information, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (in Finland, the Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman).

Children

The app is intended for healthcare professionals and students. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date. Because the app collects no data, any change would only apply to future versions of the app that add new features.
