CGMSim ONE

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 November 2026

In short: CGMSim ONE runs entirely on your device. It collects no personal data, sends nothing over the internet and shares nothing with anyone. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Who we are

CGMSim ONE is developed by Lorenzo Sandini, Lappeenranta, Finland (“we”, “the developer”). For anything about this policy or your privacy, write to lorenzo@cgmsim.com.

What the app is

CGMSim ONE is an educational simulator of a virtual patient with type 1 diabetes. It shows how food, insulin and activity move a simulated glucose curve. It is not a medical device: it does not connect to real sensors, pumps or health records, and it must not be used to dose real insulin or to make decisions about anyone’s health.

Data we collect

None. The app does not send any information to us or to anyone else. It contains no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or other third-party data-collecting software, and it does not use advertising or device identifiers to track you across apps or websites.

Data stored on your device

To run the simulation, the app keeps the following in its private storage on your device:

  • the virtual patient’s profile: name, age, sex, weight, height and activity level;
  • the virtual patient’s therapy setup: pump or injections, insulin types, basal schedule, target range and similar settings;
  • the treatments you log (carbohydrates, insulin) and the simulated glucose readings;
  • your app preferences: units, theme, chart style and notification settings.

This describes a virtual patient. You can choose any values you like, and we ask you not to enter a real person’s name or health information.

The developer has no access to this data. It never leaves the device, except as part of a device backup you have turned on (iCloud Backup on iPhone, or Android backup to your Google account), which Apple or Google handle under their own privacy policies.

Permissions

Notifications (optional). If you allow them, the app shows alerts about the simulation, such as a simulated low or high glucose, sensor expiry, a low pump reservoir or a missed basal injection. They are scheduled locally on your device. No push-notification service or server is involved, and you can turn them off at any time in the app’s Settings or in your device settings.

The app does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, photos, contacts, or health data (Apple Health or Health Connect).

Keeping and deleting your data

Data stays on your device until you remove it. To delete all of it, uninstall the app, or on Android go to Settings › Apps › CGMSim ONE › Storage › Clear storage. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

App stores and device diagnostics

You download the app from Apple’s App Store or Google Play. Apple and Google process data about your download and purchase under their own privacy policies.

If you have chosen to share diagnostics with app developers, Apple or Google may give us aggregated, anonymised crash reports and performance statistics. This data is collected by them, not by the app, and does not identify you. You can turn it off at any time:

  • iPhone: Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements (Apple Privacy Policy);
  • Android: the “Usage & diagnostics” setting in your Google settings (Google Privacy Policy).

Links that leave the app

Tapping a link in the app, such as this policy or our email address, opens your browser or email app. What happens there is covered by the privacy policies of those apps and websites. This website does not use cookies or analytics; it only remembers your Light / Dark choice in your own browser.

Contacting us

If you email us, we receive your email address and your message. We use them only to reply to you (our legitimate interest in answering your request, GDPR Article 6(1)(f)), do not share them with anyone, and delete them when they are no longer needed for that.

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation you have the right to access, correct or delete this information, to object to its processing, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority; in Finland that is the Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman.

Children

CGMSim ONE is made for learning about diabetes: students, healthcare professionals, and people living with diabetes and their families. It is not directed at children. It collects no personal data from anyone, children included.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date. If a future version of the app ever collected data, this policy would be updated before that version is released.
